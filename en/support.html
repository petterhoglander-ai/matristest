Help with the app

Restoring the purchase

If you bought the app and reinstalled it, or changed phone, open Settings in the menu (…) and tap Restore purchase. You must be signed in with the same Apple account as when you bought it. The statistics are stored on the phone only and do not come along.

Changing language

Tap SV or EN at the top of the start screen, or choose the language in Settings. The app switches right away.

The level

The level 1–10 is the app's own scale. It says how hard the tasks you solve in the app are, not how you will do in another test. When practising, the level can follow how it goes, or stay at a level you set.

Resetting the statistics

Under Settings → Reset statistics. History and rule statistics are deleted, and this cannot be undone. The purchase is not affected.

Turning off usage statistics

Under Settings → Share anonymous usage statistics. Off means nothing is sent.

Privacy

Your results stay on the phone. Read the privacy policy.
