Rule type in matrix reasoning tests

Covariation

How the rule works

Two properties are linked: the more figures, the smaller they are, or the larger the figure, the more lines along the edge. The properties need not follow any direction in the matrix; the link between them is the rule.

It resembles a conditional rule but applies to a scale rather than a pair. If you know the number, you know the size. The options in such a task often break the link with a figure that is right in number but wrong in size.

Rules in the example

  • The number of figures and their size are linked: the more figures, the smaller they are.
  • Everything else is the same in every cell.

How to recognise it

  1. Do two things change at once between cells, without either following the row?
  2. Order the cells by one property. Does the other follow, up or down?
  3. Decide one property in the empty cell and derive the other from the link.
