Rule type in matrix reasoning tests

Nested figures

How the rule works

One figure lies inside another, and the inner one follows a rule of its own: it changes shape row by row, or is distributed as three values over rows and columns. The outer one may follow another rule at the same time, or stand still.

The task therefore has two layers, and they must be read separately. Anyone who sees the cell as a single figure finds no rule at all. Start with the outer one, then the inner one, and check the options against both.

How to recognise it

  1. Is there a figure inside another? Read the outer shape on its own first.
  2. Then read the inner one: does it change shape along the row, or are three shapes distributed over the matrix?
  3. The answer must match in both layers. The options often differ only in the inner one.
